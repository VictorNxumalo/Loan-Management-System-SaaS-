How Lenders & Borrowers Use the LMS

A step-by-step walkthrough of both user journeys — every screen, what each button does, and where each action takes you. All screenshots were captured from the running application with realistic demo data.

Lender workspace · /dashboard Borrower portal · /borrower 29 annotated screens

1The Two Portals

The LMS serves two distinct audiences from one landing page. Which portal you see is decided by your account type, chosen at registration and enforced everywhere afterwards — a borrower who types a lender URL is bounced back to the borrower portal, and vice versa.

Lender — runs a lending business. Works in the Lending workspace (/dashboard): manages the people they lend to, creates loans, records repayments, reviews incoming applications, and exports reports. Staff have roles: Admin Loan officer Viewer
Borrower — an individual on the platform. Works in the Borrower portal (/borrower): browses public lenders, connects to organisations, submits loan applications, and tracks their own loans read-only.

2Signing Up & Logging In

2.1Registration — /auth/register

One form serves both account types. The "I lend money" / "I want to borrow" toggle decides which journey you enter. Required: full name, email, and a password of at least 8 characters.

2.2Login — /auth/login

After signing in, a post-login resolver (/auth/post-login) routes you automatically:

Sign in ├─ Borrower, first time → /borrower/onboarding (profile setup) ├─ Borrower, returning → /borrower (borrower home) ├─ Lender, first time → /onboarding (workspace setup) └─ Lender, returning → /dashboard (lending workspace)
If you opened a lender's invite link, login first redirects to /borrower/invites/accept so the connection is completed before anything else.

3Lender — Setting Up Your Workspace

First login as a lender lands on /onboarding. This creates your organisation — the container for all your borrowers, loans and team members.

  • The person who completes onboarding becomes the organisation's Admin.
  • Defaults (currency, interest method) pre-fill the new-loan form later; they can be changed per loan.

6Lender — Creating a Loan

6.1Loan list — /dashboard/loans

6.2New loan — /dashboard/loans/new

The form follows a deliberate preview-before-save flow: you must see the full repayment schedule before the loan can be created.

Draft → Active: a new loan starts as a Draft so you can double-check terms. Click Activate loan on the detail page to lock in the schedule and start tracking repayments.

7Lender — Loan Detail & Recording Repayments

  • Repayments are insert-only — they can't be edited or deleted afterwards, which keeps the books trustworthy. Every entry is audit-logged.
  • When total payments cover the schedule, the daily sweep marks the loan Completed; missed due dates flip it to In arrears automatically.
  • The borrower sees the same repayment history and schedule (read-only) in their portal the moment you record a payment.

8Lender — Reviewing Loan Applications

When connected borrowers apply to your organisation, applications land in /dashboard/applications and you're notified (bell + email).

Approve ──► creates a borrower CRM record (if needed) ──► creates a Draft loan with the agreed terms ──► "Open draft loan" button appears → review → Activate ──► borrower is notified and sees the loan under "My loans" Reject ──► application marked Rejected, your reason is shown to the borrower

11Borrower — Finding & Connecting to Lenders

You can't apply to a lender until you're connected to them. There are two ways in:

PathHow it works
MarketplaceBrowse the public directory and click Connect on any listed lender
InviteA lender emails you a private link → you log in → /borrower/invites/accept → Accept invite

12Borrower — Applying for a Loan

Applications always start from My lenders → Apply for loan, so the target lender is already chosen when the form opens.

You can have only one pending application per lender at a time — withdraw or wait for a decision before applying again.

13Borrower — Tracking Your Loans

Borrowers can't record payments themselves — payments happen outside the platform (EFT, cash) and the lender records them. The portal is your single source of truth for what's been captured and what's still owed.

15The End-to-End Lifecycle

How the two journeys interlock, from first contact to a fully repaid loan:

BORROWER LENDER Register + onboard Browse marketplace ───connect───► (or: Settings → invite borrower by email) Apply for loan ───────────► Applications queue 🔔 Review → Approve (set rate) or Reject 🔔 ◄──────approved────── Draft loan created → Activate My loans: schedule visible Loan detail: record repayments as money arrives 🔔 reminder (due in 3d) Dashboard: upcoming / overdue tables 🔔 ◄──────overdue─────── Daily sweep flags In arrears Loan completed ✓ Loan completed ✓ → statement PDF, CSV exports
