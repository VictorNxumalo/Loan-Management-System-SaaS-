How Lenders & Borrowers Use the LMS

A step-by-step walkthrough of both user journeys — navigation, internal wallets, profile-based KYC, simplified loan applications, wallet repayments, and where each action takes you. Screenshots were captured from the running app; some captions reflect UI updates since the images were taken.

Lender workspace · /dashboard Borrower portal · /borrower Wallets · disburse & repay in-app Profile KYC · edit per section Amounts in Rand (R)

1The Two Portals

The LMS serves two distinct audiences from one landing page. Which portal you see is decided by your account type, chosen at registration and enforced everywhere afterwards — a borrower who types a lender URL is bounced back to the borrower portal, and vice versa.

Lender — runs a lending business. Works in the Lending workspace (/dashboard): overview KPIs (including Available funds wallet balance), manages borrowers, creates and disburses loans, reviews applications with profile-attached ID documents, and collects repayments into the org wallet. Staff have roles: Admin Loan officer Viewer
Borrower — an individual on the platform. Works in the Borrower portal (/borrower): completes a KYC profile once (ID, address, bank account), browses lenders, applies with profile data auto-attached, tracks loans on the overview home, and repays via Pay from wallet (primary) or reports external bank payments (fallback).
Navigation: On wide screens, primary links sit in the top bar with a More menu for secondary items. On phones and tablets, tap the menu icon (☰) for a full-screen drawer. Page titles and action buttons stack cleanly on small screens.

2Signing Up & Logging In

2.1Registration — /auth/register

One form serves both account types. The "I lend money" / "I want to borrow" toggle decides which journey you enter. Required: full name, email, and a password of at least 8 characters.

2.2Login — /auth/login

After signing in, a post-login resolver (/auth/post-login) routes you automatically:

Sign in ├─ Borrower, first time → /borrower/onboarding (profile setup) ├─ Borrower, returning → /borrower (borrower home) ├─ Lender, first time → /onboarding (workspace setup) └─ Lender, returning → /dashboard (lending workspace)
If you opened a lender's invite link, login first redirects to /borrower/invites/accept so the connection is completed before anything else.

3Lender — Setting Up Your Workspace

First login as a lender lands on /onboarding. This creates your organisation — the container for all your borrowers, loans and team members.

  • The person who completes onboarding becomes the organisation's Admin.
  • Defaults (currency, interest method) pre-fill the new-loan form later; they can be changed per loan.

4Lender — Dashboard, Wallet & Reports

/dashboard is the home screen: clickable KPI tiles link to loans, wallet, and related pages. Available funds reflects your organisation wallet balance (top-ups, disbursements, and repayments). Collected this month sums repayments recorded this calendar month — both in-app wallet payments and confirmed external payments credit the wallet.

4.1Organisation wallet — /dashboard/wallet

Each lender organisation has an internal wallet linked to a bank account. Fund it via demo top-up (staging), use it to disburse activated loans to borrowers, and receive repayments back into available funds.

  • Disburse — on an active loan detail page, after activation, disburse moves principal from lender wallet → borrower wallet.
  • Repayments — borrower wallet payments and confirmed external payments increase available funds automatically.

6Lender — Creating a Loan

6.1Loan list — /dashboard/loans

6.2New loan — /dashboard/loans/new

The form follows a deliberate preview-before-save flow: you must see the full repayment schedule before the loan can be created.

Draft → Active: a new loan starts as a Draft so you can double-check terms. Click Activate loan on the detail page to lock in the schedule and start tracking repayments.

7Lender — Loan Detail, Repayments & Borrower Payments

7.1In-app wallet repayments

When a borrower uses Pay from wallet, the repayment is recorded immediately — no proof review. Your organisation wallet is credited and the loan balance updates. This appears in Collected this month and Available funds.

7.2External bank payments — /dashboard/payment-submissions/[id]

If a borrower paid outside the app (EFT, cash, etc.), they use Report bank payment, upload proof, and submit for review. Lender staff receive a bell notification. Open the submission, review proof, then Confirm & record (creates a repayment and credits your wallet) or Reject with a reason.

  • Repayments you record directly are insert-only — they can't be edited or deleted afterwards. Every entry is audit-logged.
  • When total payments cover the schedule, the daily sweep marks the loan Completed; missed due dates flip it to In arrears automatically.
  • The borrower sees repayment history and schedule (read-only) in their portal; confirmed pay-lender submissions appear in history after you confirm.

8Lender — Reviewing Loan Applications

When connected borrowers apply to your organisation, applications land in /dashboard/applications and you're notified (bell + email).

Approve ──► creates a borrower CRM record (if needed) ──► creates a Draft loan with the agreed terms ──► "Open draft loan" button appears → review → Activate ──► borrower is notified and sees the loan under "My loans" Reject ──► application marked Rejected, your reason is shown to the borrower

10Borrower — Setting Up Your Profile

First login lands on /borrower/onboarding — a guided flow to complete your platform profile before you can apply for loans.

11Borrower — Finding & Connecting to Lenders

You can't apply to a lender until you're connected to them. There are two ways in:

PathHow it works
MarketplaceBrowse the public directory and click Connect on any listed lender
InviteA lender emails you a private link → you log in → /borrower/invites/accept → Accept invite
One commitment at a time: while you have an open loan (draft, active, or in arrears) or any open application (draft or submitted) with a lender, you cannot start a new application elsewhere. Finish, withdraw, or wait for a decision first. If you have a draft with a lender, use Continue draft application on My lenders.

12Borrower — Applying for a Loan

Applications always start from My lenders → Apply for loan, so the target lender is already chosen when the form opens.

No duplicate uploads: ID and bank details come from your profile. Complete onboarding or visit /borrower/profile if anything is missing before applying.
You can have only one open application per lender (draft or submitted). You cannot apply while you have an active loan or another open application with any lender.

13Borrower — Tracking Loans & Repaying

  • Pay from wallet — requires sufficient wallet balance (loan disbursements credit your wallet first).
  • Report bank payment — amber banner while awaiting lender review; red banner if rejected with reason.
  • Confirmed payments appear in repayment history; wallet payments are immediate.

14Borrower & Lender — Profile & Wallet

Both portals include /borrower/profile and /dashboard/profile for identity and bank details, plus wallet pages for balances and transactions.

PagePurpose
/borrower/profile · /dashboard/profileView personal info, ID document, and bank account. Tap Edit on each section to change — fields are read-only by default to prevent accidental edits.
/borrower/walletWallet balance, transaction history, linked bank account.
/dashboard/walletOrganisation wallet, top-up (staging), disbursement and repayment ledger.
Your profile ID is attached automatically to every loan application. Keep it current if your ID document changes.

16The End-to-End Lifecycle

How the two journeys interlock, from first contact to a fully repaid loan:

BORROWER LENDER Register + complete profile (ID, bank, address) Browse marketplace ───connect───► (or: Settings → invite borrower) Apply (profile ID + bank auto) ─► Applications queue 🔔 Review checklist → Approve / Reject 🔔 ◄──────approved────── Draft loan → Activate → Disburse Wallet credited ◄─────────────── Lender wallet debited Pay from wallet ──────────► Repayment + wallet credit (instant) (or Report bank payment + proof ► Confirm → wallet credit) 🔔 reminder (due in 3d) Dashboard: collected / available funds Loan completed ✓ Loan completed ✓
